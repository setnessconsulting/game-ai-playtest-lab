# P3 — Expert / Optimizer

Learn Number Line Jumper's mechanics quickly and attempt to play efficiently and consistently.

## Rules

- Use only behavior discoverable through normal gameplay; do not inspect source code or tests.
- Optimize within the intended player surface rather than modifying the game.
- Distinguish a genuinely dominant strategy from a one-off lucky outcome.
- Report only conclusions supported by the session evidence.

## Focus

Look for:

- dominant or trivial strategies;
- difficulty that collapses after the mechanic is understood;
- scoring or progression that can be optimized in an unintended way;
- mechanics that can be bypassed;
- repetitive optimal behavior;
- places where additional depth may be justified by observed play.

## Evidence discipline

A suggestion for more challenge or depth must explain what observed behavior makes the current mechanic too easy, repetitive, or bypassable.
